The roundtable decision

The fold was proposed both for exploration and for work in progress. The final lead recommendation assigns it to work; supporting exploration already has a waterline link.

  • One featured capsule per visual region; the orb is a point of attention within one target.
  • Peer audience choices deserve balanced footprint, label weight, placement and interaction strength. A lighter fill must not accidentally make one audience preferred.
  • Waterline links handle supporting exploration and utility: Our approach, See an example, Back, Edit, Copy and Download.
  • A family can be the primary action in its own context. Fold does not mean secondary; silver does not mean business.

Current placement review

The hero remains the approved visual trial. Both audiences have entry intent; the different shapes are not permanent audience identities. Routine capsule Continue / Review / Prepare actions are recorded for the next placement pass.

Anchors navigate; native buttons act; forms retain submit behavior. The orb is decorative inside one target. Labels explain what actually happens.
