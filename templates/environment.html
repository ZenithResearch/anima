A connected home for your work.

The setup we’re designing brings three parts together.

Your work

Devgraph

Keep projects and tasks connected to the context and material they use.

Your tools

Hub

Run the tools and services that support your work in your own environment.

Your conversations

Matrix homeserver

Host your own messaging accounts and rooms, with control over how your server is run.

Choose the conversations you connect.

The goal is to make your work and its context available in the tools and conversations you choose. Each connection needs the right integration and your permission.

You don’t need your own homeserver to use an existing Matrix account or join federated rooms. Hosting one gives you control over your own messaging accounts and infrastructure. Other chat platforms need their own connections or bridges.

What setup would involve.

  1. Choose where your environment runs.
  2. Set up Devgraph and Hub.
  3. Add a Matrix homeserver if you want to host your own messaging.
  4. Choose which tools and conversations to connect, and what they may access.
