What a meaningful connection involves ＋

We begin with the desired effect, audience and creative territory. A suggested fit comes with a reason: the person’s actual work, craft and context.

Before anyone commits, both sides see the complete brief: deliverables, fee, expenses, timing, revisions and responsibilities. The creator and client choose each other, contract directly and pay directly. Anima does not hold funds or deduct a creator fee.

Creators keep ownership and approve the exact usage: channels, territory, duration and every permitted edit or derivative. A material change requires a new agreement.
